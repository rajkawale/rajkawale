## Note on naming

This project shares its name with [Trinka AI](https://www.trinka.ai), the academic-writing product it was built for. This page describes a concept prototype proposed to that team — it is not the shipped trinka.ai product, and was not released commercially.

## The Challenge

Most writing tools focus on surface-level grammar and spelling. Technical and academic writing requires deeper understanding of structure, precision, tone, and intent, which generic spell-checkers fail to address.

## The Solution

**Context-aware Precision Editing:**
Designed rules and prompts tuned for formal, technical, and academic writing structures rather than casual language.

**Focused UX/UI Prototype:**
Built an interface optimized for dense content, enabling users to review and refine complex text without cognitive overload.

## Outcome

The prototype was proposed to the Trinka AI team as a possible product direction. It did not move forward into a shipped feature, but the underlying interaction ideas — context-aware, structure-first editing rather than line-by-line grammar checking — carried into later thinking on AI-assisted writing tools.
