## Note on naming

This project shares its name with [Trinka AI](https://www.trinka.ai), the academic-writing product it was built for. This page describes a concept prototype I proposed to that team — it is not the shipped trinka.ai product, and was never released commercially.

## The Challenge

Most writing tools stop at spelling and grammar. Technical and academic writing needed something that understood structure and intent — and nothing on the market was built for that.

## The Solution

I designed the editing rules around formal, technical writing specifically, not general prose. And I built the interface for people reviewing dense paragraphs, not quick tweets — because the two need completely different UX.

## What Actually Happened

It didn't ship. That's the honest ending. But the core idea — edit for structure and intent, not just grammar — was the right one, and it's the same instinct that shows up in how I think about every writing and thinking tool since.
