## The Problem

AI is a black box even to the people building it — nobody fully knows how it reasons or where it's headed. The common response is to hand thinking over to it anyway: ask, get an answer, skip the reasoning.

## What KOS Does

KOS does the opposite. Before it answers, it forces a committed position and a probability. Then it scores that position against what actually happened — not against how helpful the answer felt.

## Status

Independent project. Currently one user: its founder. Built on evenings and weekends alongside a full-time role, to find where human thinking and AI reasoning actually combine, not where one replaces the other.

## Why It Matters

Most AI products optimize for the feeling of being helped. KOS optimizes for whether the thinking was actually right — a different bet, and the one worth testing first, on myself.
