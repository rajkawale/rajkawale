We've spent billions teaching machines how to think.

But how much are we spending on understanding how we think?

We keep making LLMs smarter while barely understanding the full potential of the human brain.

I still believe the human brain is far more capable than any LLM we've built.

Maybe the next big intelligence breakthrough isn't AI vs. humans.

Maybe it's understanding ourselves better. And how the two can amplify each other.
