Everyone is busy showing their best life.
Best version. Best smile. Best everything.

But the person who stopped performing, society calls him mad, rude, disconnected.

And the person everyone calls sorted, happy, stable — sit with him once.

Just once.

The one who accepted himself does not need your validation.

The one performing for you does not even know himself.

Most people are not comfortable with their own worst.

So they hide it behind their best.

— Raj
